# Audiences

Only the audiences below are marketed to. Copy the section once per audience. Every figure under "Pains" carries a source and a date; an unverified figure is never quoted in an ad.

## Members

**Who they are:** Adults 25–45 who live or work near one of the three gyms, train irregularly or have lapsed from a big-box gym, and want coaching without a personal-trainer price.

**Geo:** Greater Manchester (radius targeting around the three gyms; locations "living in or recently in").

**Language:** English.

**Pains:**
- big gyms feel anonymous and crowded at peak times (12 member interviews by the Operator, 2026-09; no figures to quote)
- classes are full or cancelled (same interviews, 2026-09)
- people do not know what to do on the gym floor (same interviews, 2026-09)
- contracts are hard to leave (same interviews, 2026-09)

**Must not be addressed as:** anything about body shape, weight, health conditions, age or appearance ("are you overweight?", "beach body", "after your pregnancy").

**Excluded audiences:** current members (a customer-list upload waits for the ruling in `config/claims.md`; until then, excluded by the pixel event `MemberLogin` in the last 180 days).

**Special ad category:** NONE (gym memberships are not housing, credit, employment or politics; United Kingdom).

## Corporate

**Who they are:** HR managers, people-and-culture leads and office managers at Manchester employers with 20–250 staff, looking for a wellbeing perk their staff will use.

**Geo:** Greater Manchester.

**Language:** English.

**Pains:**
- wellbeing budgets with low uptake (5 calls with existing corporate clients, 2026-10; no figures to quote)
- perks staff do not use (same calls, 2026-10)
- no easy way to show usage to leadership (same calls, 2026-10)

**Must not be addressed as:** anything implying staff health problems, absence or productivity claims ("cut sick days").

**Excluded audiences:** current corporate clients' staff (no list uploaded; see `config/claims.md`).

**Special ad category:** NONE (a B2B service ad; job-title targeting is used for reach only, not an employment ad; United Kingdom).
