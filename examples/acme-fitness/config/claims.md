## Forbidden words and claims

- "guaranteed" (in any form)
- "lose X kg", "drop a dress size", or any weight or body-measurement result
- any health-outcome claim (fitter, healthier, lower blood pressure, fewer sick days, better sleep, mental-health benefits)
- "best gym in Manchester" or any superlative without a source
- prices or discounts not shown on the destination page

## Lines that need clearance before use in ads

- "Cancel anytime." (FAQ, /join): the Owner confirms the membership terms support it in an ad before use.

## Legal jurisdiction notes

> Verify with your lawyer. These notes are perishable and dated; re-check before each launch.

Illustrative notes for a fictional company, dated 2026-10-26; verify with your lawyer.

- **United Kingdom, advertising:** the CAP Code (ASA) applies to Meta ads in the UK: claims must be substantiated, "free" must mean free (the trial needs no payment details), and health or weight claims for fitness services are high-risk. Verify with your lawyer.
- **United Kingdom, marketing email and SMS:** UK GDPR and PECR require prior consent (or the soft opt-in for existing customers) before marketing email or SMS; the trial-form consent covers contact about the trial, not general marketing. Verify with your lawyer.
- **Open question for counsel:** whether member or lead lists may be uploaded to Meta as custom audiences (see the last section). Verify with your lawyer.
