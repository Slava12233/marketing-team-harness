# Brand

## Colours

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| `#1F2A36` | Charcoal: primary brand colour, backgrounds, logo, text on orange |
| `#FF6B35` | Ember orange: accent only (one highlight word or shape per creative) |
| `#FFFFFF` | White: ink on charcoal, page background |

## Fonts

Inter (400, 600, 800), on the website and in ads. One typeface, Latin script only; weight carries the hierarchy.

## Logo rules

Single-fill wordmark "ACME FITNESS" in charcoal or white (files from the web team, see `config/product.md`); never recoloured orange, stretched, outlined or placed on a busy photo without a charcoal band. Minimum clear space: the height of the "A".

## Tone of voice

Warm, direct, a little dry. Second person ("you"), informal. Short sentences. Coaches, not drill sergeants: "Come and try a class" yes, "No excuses" no. Honest about what a class is like; no hype, no shaming.

## Languages and direction

| Language | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| English | LTR | the only ad language; both destination pages are English |

## RTL rules

none

## AI-imagery disclosure

- Any generated or AI-altered image or video carries a small caption "AI image" in the bottom corner (white on charcoal, at least 24 px on a 1080 px frame).
- Generated media never shows a real member, coach or one of the three real gyms as if it were real.
- Ad-level AI self-disclosure is set to OPT_IN on every ad that contains generated media.

## Do not do

- No before-and-after photos.
- No bodies shown as a goal; show people training, not physiques.
- No stock "fitness model" imagery; real class photos first.
- No more than one orange element per creative.
- No text on the image that repeats the headline.
- No button drawn on the image (Meta renders the ad's button).
