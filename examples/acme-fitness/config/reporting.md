# Client reporting

How the weekly client report (the `client-report` skill, `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-client-report.md` plus its rendered HTML and, on request, PDF) is written and who reads it. The harness writes and renders the report; it never sends it. The Owner reads it first, then the Operator sends it through the channel below.

**Report language:** English

**Report direction:** ltr

**Cadence:** weekly, Monday at 09:30 Europe/London, before the weekly round at 10:00, covering the previous Monday–Sunday

**Recipient:** Jordan (fictional), founder and Owner

**Channel:** email from the Operator with the PDF attached, plus the short version as a WhatsApp text on the same morning

## Sections the client wants

- The three-sentence answer
- The results table: trials started for Members, booked calls for Corporate wellness
- What we changed this week
- What we recommend
- What we need from you
- Money against the caps in `config/goals.md`, one line
- The short version for WhatsApp

## Never include

- Ad, ad set, campaign, account, pixel and form ids
- Agent, skill, tool and model names; file paths
- Internal notes, the competitor scan and draft creatives that were not built
- Any member's or lead's name, email or phone
- Cost per click (the founder asked to see cost per trial and cost per call only, with CTR as the one click figure)

**Compare to:** both: the previous week and the target cost per result in `config/goals.md`

**Currency format:** £1,234.50 (pound sign before the amount, two decimals, comma for thousands)
