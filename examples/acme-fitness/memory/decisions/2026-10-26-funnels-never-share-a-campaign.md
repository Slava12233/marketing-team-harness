# The two funnels never share a campaign

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.

**Ruling.** Members and Corporate wellness are always separate campaigns, each optimised for its own result (a trial started; a booked call) and each under its own per-campaign cap.

**Context.** Asked at `/setup` (round (i)); a mixed campaign would report "Multiple conversions" at the campaign level and blur the cost per result ([[2026-10-25-mixed-optimisation-goals-show-multiple-conversions]]).

**What it replaces.** Nothing; first ruling on structure.

**Applies to.** [[2026-11-MEMBERS-Trial]], [[2026-11-CORPORATE-Call]] and every later flight.
