# The Owner pauses; the harness pauses only when asked

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.

**Ruling.** A breakage found by `/morning` (a disapproval, a broken link, a pixel failure) is reported with a click list and the Owner pauses; the harness pauses only when the Owner asks in chat. `/morning` never writes to the account.

**Context.** Round (h) of `/setup`; recorded in `config/operator.md` ("Who clicks what").

**What it replaces.** Nothing.

**Applies to.** Every campaign.
