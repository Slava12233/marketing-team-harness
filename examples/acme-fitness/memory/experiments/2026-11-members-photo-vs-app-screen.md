# 2026-11-members-photo-vs-app-screen

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE. Nothing is created in Meta; experiments start only on the Owner's go.

## Pre-registration (frozen on the day of launch)
- **Hypothesis:** a real class photo (#1 Class-Floor) gets a lower cost per trial than the app screenshot (#2 App-Pass), because the members' interviews say big gyms feel anonymous and small classes are the draw.
- **Variable (one):** the image; same primary text family, headline, audience, placements and budget split.
- **Control / treatment:** #2 App-Pass (control) / #1 Class-Floor (treatment).
- **Primary metric and window:** cost per `StartTrial`, 7-day click attribution.
- **Decision rule:** if #1's cost per trial is at least 20 % lower with at least 15 trials per arm, #1 takes the budget; guardrail: frequency under 3; null result: keep both and test the hook next.
- **Minimum events and duration:** 15 trials per arm and at least 7 days; not before the first week of delivery (risk appetite in [[business/goals|goals]]).
