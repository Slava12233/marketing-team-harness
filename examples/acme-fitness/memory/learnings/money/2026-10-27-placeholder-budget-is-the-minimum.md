# A paused build carries the minimum budget as a placeholder

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.

**The fact.** Both campaigns were built with the account's minimum daily budget, 1.00 GBP, as a placeholder (it spends nothing while PAUSED). The Owner's proposed go for Members, £20 a day with a £300 cap, stays under every cap: Meta's weekly limit makes it at most £140 a week against the £420 weekly cap ([[2026-10-21-weekly-cap-is-seven-times-daily-budget]]).

**Evidence.** The read-back of 2026-10-27 17:10 Europe/London and the caps in [[business/goals|goals]].

**How to apply.** Never activate a placeholder budget: the go names the daily budget and the cap, and the budget is changed in the same step as the activation, then read back.
