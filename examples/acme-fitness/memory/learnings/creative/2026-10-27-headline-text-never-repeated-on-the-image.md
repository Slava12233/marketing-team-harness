# The image never repeats the headline

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.

**The fact.** #5 Usage-Report first carried "Book a 20-minute call" on the card, the same words as its headline; the brand's do-not-do list ([[business/brand|brand]]) and the hook rule (visual and headline must not repeat each other) sent it back once. The built card shows a usage report by headcount; the headline asks for the call.

**Evidence.** The creative review before the build on 2026-10-27 ([[2026-10-27]]).

**How to apply.** Check every type-led card against its headline before compliance; one idea on the image, the ask in the headline and Meta's button.
