# Claims and compliance

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE. The legal notes are illustrative only. Filled by `/setup` from `config/claims.md` on 2026-10-26.

## Claims

- **Allowed (each with its source and date):** "three gyms across Manchester" (/join, 2026-10-26); "7-day free trial, no card" (/join, 2026-10-26); "small-group classes" (/join, 2026-10-26); "a 20-minute call" (/corporate/call, 2026-10-26).
- **Forbidden words and claims:** "guaranteed"; any weight or body-measurement result; any health-outcome claim; "best gym in Manchester" or any unsourced superlative; prices or discounts not shown on the destination page.
- **Lines that need clearance before use in ads:** "Cancel anytime." (the Owner confirms the membership terms support it).

## Consent and disclosure wording

- `join-notice-v1` (the /join trial form) and `corporate-notice-v1` (the /corporate/call booking form), quoted by id only.

## Marketing messages and lists

- Marketing email or SMS only with prior consent (UK GDPR and PECR; verify with your lawyer); the trial-form consent covers contact about the trial. No member, lead or client list is uploaded to Meta until the Owner rules after counsel.

## Special ad categories

- United Kingdom, Members: NONE. United Kingdom, Corporate: NONE (a B2B service ad, not an employment ad).

## Personal attributes

- Never address the viewer by religion, origin, health, family status or other personal attributes; for Acme also never by body shape, weight, age or appearance.

## AI imagery

- Any generated or AI-altered image or video carries the caption "AI image" (white on charcoal, at least 24 px on a 1080 px frame); generated media never shows a real member, coach or gym as real; ad-level AI self-disclosure OPT_IN ([[2026-10-25-ai-self-disclosure-is-an-object]]).

## Open legal items

- Whether member or lead lists may become custom audiences (UK GDPR): the Owner, after counsel. Verify with your lawyer.
