# Audiences

> FICTIONAL EXAMPLE. Filled by `/setup` from `config/audiences.md` on 2026-10-26.

## Who we market to

- **Members:** adults 25–45 who live or work near one of the three gyms, train irregularly or have lapsed from a big-box gym; Greater Manchester (radius around the gyms, "living in or recently in"); English; special ad category NONE.
- **Corporate:** HR managers, people-and-culture leads and office managers at Manchester employers with 20–250 staff; English; special ad category NONE (a B2B service ad, not an employment ad).
- Not marketed to: anyone outside Greater Manchester; current members (excluded by `MemberLogin`, 180 days).

## Pains

- Members (12 member interviews by the Operator, 2026-09; no figures to quote): big gyms feel anonymous and crowded at peak times; classes are full or cancelled; people do not know what to do on the gym floor; contracts are hard to leave.
- Corporate (5 calls with existing corporate clients, 2026-10; no figures to quote): wellbeing budgets with low uptake; perks staff do not use; no easy way to show usage to leadership.

## Per-audience notes

- Corporate uses job-title targeting for reach only, and an instant form; it waits for domain verification ([[2026-10-26-corporate-waits-for-domain-verification]]).

## How we may address them

- Members: never body shape, weight, health conditions, age or appearance.
- Corporate: never staff health problems, absence or productivity claims ("cut sick days").
