# flights/

One note per creative flight (`YYYY-MM-<topic>-flight-N.md`), made from `memory/templates/flight.md`: every creative with its fixed number (never renumbered; new ones take the next number), its format, its image hash or video id once uploaded, its status and verdict, the preview site URL and the folder under `creatives/`. Creative files are linked by path; Obsidian does not index `creatives/`.
