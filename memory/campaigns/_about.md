# campaigns/

One note per Meta campaign the harness builds or manages, named exactly as the campaign (`2026-11-MEMBERS-Trial.md`), made from `memory/templates/campaign.md`. It holds the ids (in backticks), the ad sets and ads, status, budgets, caps, dates and the links to the plan, the flight, the reports and the experiments. `## Current state` is rewritten after every change with the time of the live read in `as_of`; `## Log` is append-only. No live metrics: figures live in reports and carry `as_of` and `source`.
