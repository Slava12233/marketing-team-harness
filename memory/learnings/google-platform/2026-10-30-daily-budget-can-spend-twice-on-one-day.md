# A Google daily budget can spend up to twice on one day

**The fact.** A Google Ads campaign budget is an average: on a single day Google may spend up to twice it, and over a calendar month it keeps spend within 30.4 times the daily budget.

**Evidence.** Google Ads help on campaign budgets, read 2026-10-30. Not yet observed on a company's account: the first live call confirms it or a new note supersedes it.

**How to apply.** `google-ads.mjs` refuses a Google daily budget above half the Google share of the daily cap in `config/goals.md`, so a double day still stays inside the cap; the weekly and monthly caps are the firm ones.
