# Click-to-WhatsApp sets get no detailed targeting; Advantage+ detailed targeting is applied

**The fact.** Meta's help says detailed targeting is not available for click-to-WhatsApp and that Advantage+ detailed targeting is applied automatically to conversation-optimised sets.

**Evidence.** Observed on a single company's ad account on 2026-10-21; ported to this template on 2026-10-26.

**How to apply.** Read the set's audience panel in Ads Manager before trusting its spec; hold the audience with controls (locations, languages, minimum age, exclusions; [[2026-10-25-advantage-plus-languages-are-a-control]]).
