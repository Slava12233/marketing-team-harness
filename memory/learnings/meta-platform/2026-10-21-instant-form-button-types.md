# Instant-form ads take six button types; expect CONTACT_US to be refused

**The fact.** The lead-ads guide lists APPLY_NOW, DOWNLOAD, GET_QUOTE, LEARN_MORE, SIGN_UP and SUBSCRIBE as instant-form buttons; expect CONTACT_US to be refused.

**Evidence.** Observed on a single company's ad account on 2026-10-21; ported to this template on 2026-10-26.

**How to apply.** Instant-form ads use one of the six (LEARN_MORE or SIGN_UP fit a booked call or a sign-up); do not propose CONTACT_US on a form ad.
