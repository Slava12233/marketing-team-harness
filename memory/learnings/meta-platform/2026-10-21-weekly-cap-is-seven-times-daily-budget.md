# Meta caps spend at 7× the daily budget per Sunday–Saturday week

**The fact.** Meta's weekly cap on daily budgets (7× per Sunday–Saturday week, prorated) makes a 15-day set at a daily budget of 100 (in the account currency) cost at most 1,500 and a 14-day one 1,400; any single day may spend more than the daily budget.

**Evidence.** Observed on a single company's ad account on 2026-10-21; ported to this template on 2026-10-26.

**How to apply.** State the spend as daily budget, weekly cap (7×) and total cap before any go; a day can overspend its budget but the week cannot.
