# WhatsApp inbox: the ad card is the only per-lead attribution; read only on the Owner's ask

**The fact.** In WhatsApp Business Web every click-to-WhatsApp chat carries an ad card (headline, text, fb.me or instagram.com) above the first message, the only per-lead campaign attribution; many chats open with Meta's default pre-fill rather than the ad's `page_welcome_message` (cause unverified); push names are exposed for few leads.

**Evidence.** Observed on a single company's ad account on 2026-10-26; ported to this template on 2026-10-26.

**How to apply.** Read the inbox only on the Owner's ask, open chats by mouse only (a keyboard shortcut once sent a stray character to a lead), keep phone numbers and names out of this repository, and record counts only.
