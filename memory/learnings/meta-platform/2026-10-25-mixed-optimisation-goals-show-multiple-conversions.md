# A campaign whose ad sets optimise for different goals shows "Multiple conversions"

**The fact.** A campaign whose ad sets have different optimisation goals shows «Multiple conversions» / «—» as its campaign-level result.

**Evidence.** Observed on a single company's ad account on 2026-10-25; ported to this template on 2026-10-26.

**How to apply.** Read results at the ad-set level for mixed campaigns (for example WhatsApp + form sets).
