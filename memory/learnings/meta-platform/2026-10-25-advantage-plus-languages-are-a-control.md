# Under Advantage+ audience, languages are a control; age_max is only a suggestion

**The fact.** Under Advantage+ audience, languages are an audience CONTROL together with locations, minimum age and excluded custom audiences (Meta help `938372127764391`), so a `locales` filter holds; `age_max` is only a suggestion.

**Evidence.** Observed on a single company's ad account on 2026-10-25; ported to this template on 2026-10-26.

**How to apply.** Hold an audience with locations, `locales`, minimum age and exclusions; do not rely on `age_max` or interests to narrow.
