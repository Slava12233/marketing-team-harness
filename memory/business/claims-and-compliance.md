## Marketing messages and lists

- Israel: marketing email, SMS, automatic dialling or fax only with prior explicit consent (Communications Law §30A); a WhatsApp chat about the service is not consent to general marketing. Verify with your lawyer (2026-10-29).
- Custom audiences from company lists: not ruled, the Owner's legal question.
