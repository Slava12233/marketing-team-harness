# Client reporting

How the weekly client report (the `client-report` skill, `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-client-report.md` plus its rendered HTML and, on request, PDF) is written and who reads it. The harness writes and renders the report; it never sends it. The Owner reads it first, then the Operator sends it through the channel below.

Defaults set by /setup on 2026-10-29 without an interview round (the Owner is the reader); re-run /setup to change them.

**Report language:** Hebrew

**Report direction:** rtl

**Cadence:** weekly, Sunday morning, covering the previous Sunday–Saturday

**Recipient:** the Owner (in-house)

**Channel:** a shared folder (the HTML), plus the short version as WhatsApp text

## Sections the client wants

- the three-sentence answer
- the results table
- what we changed
- what we recommend
- what we need from you
- money against the caps
- the short version for WhatsApp

## Never include

- ad, ad set, campaign, account, pixel and form ids
- agent, skill, tool and model names
- internal notes and file paths
- secrets
- any lead's name, email or phone
- the other team's campaigns on the shared account

**Compare to:** both (the previous period and the targets in config/goals.md)

**Currency format:** ₪1,234
