## Forbidden words and claims

- guaranteed sales, sell-out, a number of units sold, or a sale within a time
- prices, yields, returns, appreciation or any financial outcome
- "מובטח" / "guaranteed", "ללא סיכון" / "risk-free", "הכי טוב" / "the best", "מספר 1" / "No. 1"
- financial, legal, tax or mortgage advice, or any licence claim (e.g. licensed broker)
- buyer counts, lead counts, results or figures without a source and a date
- any statement about the viewer's financial situation

## Legal jurisdiction notes

> Verify with your lawyer. These notes are perishable and dated; re-check before each launch.

- **Israel, 2026-10-29.** Marketing messages by email, SMS, automatic dialling or fax need the recipient's prior explicit consent (Communications Law (Telecommunications and Broadcasting), §30A, the "spam law"); a WhatsApp chat a developer opens about the service is not consent to general marketing. Verify with your lawyer.
- **Israel, 2026-10-29.** Real-estate brokerage is a licensed activity (Real Estate Brokers Law, 1996); ads must not present Habait as a broker or imply brokerage services unless the Owner confirms a licence. Verify with your lawyer.
- **Israel, 2026-10-29.** Privacy: personal data from chats stays out of this repository (Protection of Privacy Law). Verify with your lawyer.
- Open for counsel: whether "ללא עמלת תיווך" may be used, and on what basis.
