# Goals and money

The money limits the harness works within. Amounts are in the account currency (`currency` in `config/accounts.md`). `/morning` and `/sync` copy the caps below into `dashboard/state.json` (`money.caps`) and flag spend above 80 % of a cap; the first flight plan proposes budgets inside the band and below the caps, each labelled "Needs the Owner's go".

The caps cover this harness's own campaigns. The ad account is shared with another team (decision 2026-10-29): spend is reported for the whole account and for Habait's own campaigns separately.

**Monthly budget band:** ₪500–₪1,000 a month

## Target cost per result

| Funnel | Result | Target cost | Source of the target |
|---|---|---|---|
| developers-whatsapp | a conversation started | not stated yet: set after the first week of data | the Owner, 2026-10-29 |

**First funnel to launch:** developers-whatsapp: the only funnel, and the simplest conversion (a WhatsApp conversation Meta counts itself, no pixel needed).

The harness never proposes a budget above a cap; a cap change is the Owner's, in writing.

## Success, tests and review

**Success in 30 days:** The developers funnel runs 30 days inside the caps, and we know its real cost per conversation and per qualified developer.

**Risk appetite for tests:** up to 20 % of the band on experiments and new creatives

**Review cadence:** weekly, in `/next-round` (Sunday 10:00)
