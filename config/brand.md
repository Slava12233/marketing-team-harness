# Brand

## Colours

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| #041F6E | primary: navy background of the logo (read from the Page logo 2026-10-29, not confirmed) |
| #FFFFFF | wordmark and text on navy; background for navy-on-white |

The AI-disclosure badge "הדמיה" is reserved white text on #041F6E.

## Fonts

Heebo for Hebrew (proposed 2026-10-29, not confirmed), regular and bold; at most two typefaces in any ad. The thin "HABAIT" wordmark is used as the logo file only, never retyped.

## Logo rules

The HABAIT wordmark ("HABAIT / HOME IS HABAIT") white on #041F6E, or navy on white; never mirrored (also in RTL layouts), stretched, recoloured or redrawn; clear space around it at least the height of the "H". Source: the Facebook Page profile picture until the Owner supplies the logo files (they go in `creatives/src/`).

## Tone of voice

Professional, direct, concrete, calm. Peer-to-peer B2B toward developers, plural "אתכם". Do: name what the developer gets, in plain words. Don't: hype, slang, exclamation marks, promises of results, superlatives.

## Languages and direction

| Language | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hebrew | RTL | the only ad language |

## RTL rules

Right-aligned text and right-to-left layout; numbers and Latin names (HABAIT, AI, PropTech, WhatsApp) stay left-to-right inside Hebrew text; Hebrew punctuation at the visual end of the line; the logo and any video controls are never mirrored.

## AI-imagery disclosure

Every generated or altered image or video of a place, product or person carries a visible badge "הדמיה", white text on #041F6E, in a corner inside the safe zone. Generated media is never shown or captioned as a real project, address or person. The ad-level AI self-disclosure is OPT_IN whenever anything in the ad is generated.

## Do not do

- no button drawn on the image (Meta renders the ad's button)
- no dates or prices on the image
- no stock clichés (handshakes, keys, piggy banks)
- no competitor names
- no image of a specific project unless it is a Habait client's and cleared for ads
