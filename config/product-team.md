## Where product truth lives

| Need | Path |
|---|---|
| Rules, legal gate, standing rulings | n/a (no product team): `config/claims.md` and `memory/decisions/` |

## How a request is filed

A change to a page, event or notice is a dated plan file in `plans/` (`plans/YYYY-MM-DD-request-<topic>.md`) with the exact wording or spec, labelled **"Needs Owner"**. The Owner reads it in the session and carries it out himself.
