# Audiences

Only the audiences below are marketed to. Copy the section once per audience. Every figure under "Pains" carries a source and a date; an unverified figure is never quoted in an ad.

## Real-estate developers

**Who they are:** Owners and managers of small and mid-size residential development companies in Israel who are planning or selling a project and want buyers for its units.

**Geo:** Israel (all regions)

**Language:** Hebrew

**Pains:**
- presales that move slowly before permit and financing milestones (Owner's draft, 2026-10-29; unverified, no figure)
- marketing agencies that cost a lot and bring weak leads (Owner's draft, 2026-10-29; unverified, no figure)
- no time in-house to run marketing (Owner's draft, 2026-10-29; unverified, no figure)

**Must not be addressed as:** never by financial status (e.g. "in trouble", "can't sell"), religion, origin, ethnicity, age, health or family status; a business framing ("for developers who want buyers for their project") is fine, an identity assertion is not.

**Excluded audiences:** home buyers and consumers looking for an apartment (not this funnel); named individuals from any list; the other team's audiences on the shared ad account.

**Special ad category:** NONE in Israel: a B2B marketing service offered to companies; the ads do not offer homes to people. Confirm in Ads Manager before the build; if an ad shows a specific apartment or project for sale, declare HOUSING.
