# /morning: the daily opener (read-only toward the account)

One fixed routine, the same shape every day, so one morning compares with the next. Project rules (`CLAUDE.md`) and the vault contract (`memory/README.md`, "who writes what", `/morning`) win on every conflict with this file. Takes about five minutes of tool calls.

**Output:** `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-morning.md`; the Inbox answers; the read written into `memory/state/current.md` and the campaign notes (a verification, not an account change); a line in today's session note; the generated files (`memory/HOT.md`, `memory/INDEX.md`, HOME's block, `dashboard/state.json`) and the setup progress page. **Language:** the report file in English; the terminal summary and the Inbox answers in the chat language from `config/operator.md`; money in the account currency from `config/accounts.md`; dates absolute, times in the timezone from `config/operator.md`.

The routine time for `/morning` (and for `/sync` and `/next-round`) is set in `config/operator.md`.

## Rules that never bend
- **Reads only toward the account.** No `ads_activate_entity`, no `ads_update_entity`, no budget, schedule, audience or creative writes, no experiment creation. When this command finds a breakage it **reports it with a click list and the Owner pauses**; the harness pauses only when the Owner asks in chat. The only writes are the vault notes named below, the report and the generated files.
- **The Inbox never acts.** A line in `memory/inbox/owner.md` is filed, answered or turned into a proposal; anything that moves money or delivery still needs the Owner's go in chat, in the same message, with the spend stated.
- Never fabricate a number. A metric the account cannot give reads "no data".
- No PII: counts by `source` and `ref` only. No secrets, names only.
- Every spend-affecting recommendation is labelled **Needs the Owner's go**; every page, event or notice change **Needs product team** (or **Needs Owner** when `config/product-team.md` says there is no product team).
- Single bounded calls; never a polling loop. If a review or a process has to be waited for, say so and stop.

## Procedure
0. **Inbox first.** Read `memory/inbox/owner.md`. Under every open line write an indented `↳` bullet in the chat language: done, with the note it went to; needs your go, with the cost stated; or filed to `[[note]]`. File each fact into the right note per the `memory-update` skill (a ruling → a decision note; a finding → a learning note after a grep of `memory/INDEX.md`; an account fact → the entity note). A line that would change spend, budget, schedule, an audience or delivery becomes a **Needs the Owner's go** item in section 8 of the report and is never acted on. Answered lines move to the answered section; the Owner's own words are never edited.
1. **Health first.** Run the `health` skill's checks (it is read-only). Its green/yellow/red table becomes section 1 of the report. A red item does not stop the morning run; it is stated at the top and the run continues with what works.
2. **Memory.** `memory/HOT.md` (injected by the SessionStart hook; read it if its lines are not in context), `memory/state/current.md` (account facts, the campaigns table, the next actions, the plan to read first), the newest session note under `memory/sessions/YYYY-MM/`, `memory/maps/build.md` and the `memory/learnings/meta-platform/` notes it lists, the campaign notes in `memory/campaigns/` for the campaigns in the table, the decision notes that set the decision rules, the plan `memory/state/current.md` points to, and, when `config/product-team.md` names one, the product team's record of what it shipped or ruled since (launch-day switches, notices, counsel, page changes), read-only.
3. **Checklist.** Read `checklist.md`: the table (`id`, `item`, `owner`, `status`, `note`, `updated`) and the newest line of `## Sync log`. Group the `open` and `blocked` items by owner (Owner, Operator, Product team, Harness; items that wait on outside counsel are owned by the Owner). If `checklist.md` is missing, say so in red and fall back to the vault notes.
4. **Live account.** With `ads_get_ad_entities` (`ad_account_id` = `meta_ad_account_id` from `config/accounts.md`, `object_type`, `object_ids`, `fields`):
   - every campaign in the campaigns table of `memory/state/current.md`, including campaigns not made by this harness that a decision note says to leave running: `status`, `effective_status`, `daily_budget`, `amount_spent`;
   - their ad sets: `status`, `effective_status`, `daily_budget`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `delivery` (sub-status: `in_review`, `pending_process`, `scheduled`, `off`), ad counts;
   - their ads: counts by `effective_status` (PAUSED, ACTIVE, PENDING_REVIEW, DISAPPROVED, WITH_ISSUES, IN_PROCESS); list every ad that is not PAUSED or ACTIVE by name;
   - `ads_get_errors` (`entity_ids`: the campaign ids) must be empty;
   - `ads_account_get_activity_logs` (`ad_account_id`; `start_time` = the time of the last line in the newest session note and `end_time` = now, both ISO 8601; optional `object_id`, `user_id`, `limit` 1–1000): read the `event_category` values `budget`, `status` and `targeting` first (one call each), then the rest unfiltered. The log comes back in the account's language; `run_status` 1 = active, 9 = pending review, 17 = pending publish; a whole campaign's log can exceed the tool-output limit, so filter the saved file with jq. A change by a hand other than this harness (another user, an app, or Meta's own system changes) is a **finding** in section 2, with what changed, from and to, when and by which account label;
   - anything whose `effective_status` is ACTIVE while its campaign note says PAUSED is a **red finding**, reported, not touched;
   - **lead funnels:** the count of leads comes from the live read (the ad set's results, yesterday and flight-to-date; in the live phase, the analyst's table), never from lead rows: the harness has no tool that reads them and never copies a name, an email or a phone number. The report says where the Operator reads the leads themselves: in Meta Business Suite's Leads Center (`docs/meta-setup-guide.md` has the click path), or in the company's own system when `config/accounts.md` holds a `lead_webhook_url`. Leads arriving while the `checklist.md` row that confirms Leads Center notifications is still open is a finding: nobody may be seeing them.
   - **Then write what the read showed** (a verification, not an account change): set `as_of` in `memory/state/current.md` to the time of this read (`source: ads_get_ad_entities`) and correct any line the read contradicts; rewrite each read campaign note's `## Current state` from the read, with `as_of`, and append a `## Log` line only where something differed from the note (a dated correction naming the finding). No live metrics go into the notes; they stay in the report.
4b. **Meta's own diagnostics (reads).** After the entity read and the activity log, so Meta's view of "abnormal" and "below benchmark" sits next to the harness's own rules. Every call carries `client_conversation_id`, `client_model`, `advertiser_job` (`assess_and_optimize_performance`, one value for the whole run) and `advertiser_request`, and `ad_account_id` = `meta_ad_account_id` from `config/accounts.md`. The date parameters are either `date_preset` (`today`, `yesterday`, `last_2_days`, `last_7d`, `last_14d`, `last_28d`, `last_30d`, `this_week`, `last_week`, `this_month`, `last_month`, `lifetime`) or `date_from` + `date_to` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), never both.
   - **Anomalies:** `ads_insights_anomaly_signal` once on the account (no `entity_ids`), then once with `entity_ids` = the ACTIVE campaigns' ids (all ids of one entity type). These are observations of a spike or a drop, not causes: report the entity, the metric and the direction.
   - **Peers:** `ads_insights_performance_vs_peers` with `entity_ids` = every ACTIVE ad set, `date_preset` `last_7d`, and in the live phase a second call with `yesterday`. Leave `analysis_metric` out (the tool then judges on the result the ad set optimises for); pass `CPR`, `CTR`, `CPM`, `CPC` or `ROAS` only when a question names that metric. Percentiles are direction-aware (higher is always better, cost metrics included) and each ad set has its own cohort: compare within one ad set, never across. Lead with the main KPI (cost per result); the other metrics explain it as funnel diagnostics.
   - **Industry benchmark:** `ads_insights_industry_benchmark` per ACTIVE ad set (`entity_ids`, `date_preset` `last_7d`), with `optimization_goal_override` matching that ad set's optimisation goal (for example `AD_OPTIMIZATION_GOAL_LEAD_GENERATION`, `_REPLIES`, `_OFFSITE_CONVERSIONS`, `_LANDING_PAGE_VIEWS`) so the cohort is like for like; `sub_vertical` only when `config/company.md` states the company's industry sub-vertical in Meta's words, never inferred; `cas_segment` and `analysis_metric` left out. Above the benchmark is always the better standing: for a cost metric, an ad set above the benchmark pays less than its peers, and its line reads "below benchmark" in cost.
   - **Auction:** `ads_insights_auction_ranking_benchmarks` (`entity_ids` = the ACTIVE ad sets, `date_preset` `last_7d`) only when two or more ad sets are ACTIVE: which ads rank stronger, the bid and ad-quality factors, and auction overlap between ad sets (overlap → a consolidation recommendation per the `scaling-playbook`, labelled **Needs the Owner's go**).
   - **Opportunity score:** `ads_get_opportunity_score` once per morning, after `ads_get_ad_entities`, never first. It is account-level only (never a campaign's or an ad set's score); the recommendations are a subset, sorted by `opportunity_score_lift` in points, each with a `lift_estimate` and a `url` to review in Ads Manager. A recommendation is reported, never applied: applying one is an account change and **Needs the Owner's go**.
   - A tool that answers "not available", an error or an empty result (common on a small or new account) gives the cell "no data", never a guess. Pre-launch (nothing ACTIVE): only the account-level anomaly signal and the opportunity score run; the table reads "no ACTIVE ad set". These readouts go into the report only, never into a vault note as figures.
5. **Phase.**
   - **Pre-launch (no ad set ACTIVE):** sections 2–8 below; spend reads "0 in the account currency, everything PAUSED"; the recommendations are about readiness (gates, wording decisions, counsel, product-team items, render checks).
   - **Live (an ad set ACTIVE):** delegate the numbers to the `performance-analyst` agent (sonnet) with the `meta-performance-report` skill: yesterday vs the previous equal period, flight-to-date vs plan, the ad ranking, the diagnosis order, the decision rules from the flight plan and the decision notes. Embed its summary table and its recommendations; its file stays its own (`reports/YYYY-MM-DD-performance.md`), and its proposed note changes arrive in `memory/inbox/proposals.md` for `/sync`.
   - **A switch day** (a launch date or a destination change named in the plan or in `config/company.md`, and the day after): also confirm the destination pages from `config/product.md` load in the ad's language, that the ad sets' schedule and the weekly cap behave as planned, and that any CTA the plan says disappears on that day is gone.
   - **Every phase: money vs caps** (the logic of `health` check 15). Read the hard caps in `config/goals.md` (its caps table, columns Cap, Amount, Set by: daily, weekly, monthly, per campaign). Compare yesterday's spend with the daily cap, this week's (Sunday–Saturday, Meta's budget week, unless `config/goals.md` defines it otherwise) with the weekly cap, this month's with the monthly cap, each campaign's spend with its own cap, and the sum of the live daily budgets (converted from the minor unit) with the daily cap; spend only from a live read (step 4's objects, and `ads_insights_performance_trend` for yesterday, this week and this month). A figure at 80 % of a cap or more is a flagged line in the summary labelled **Needs the Owner's attention**; a figure above a cap is a red line with the pause click list from the breakage table (the Owner pauses). Meta may spend more than a daily budget on one day within its weekly ceiling: say so when the week is still under its cap. `config/goals.md` missing or holding placeholders: one yellow line, "no caps set: run /setup round (i)".
6. **Recommend.** Three to five items, ranked, each with evidence → action → label. Cover three points of view: creative (fatigue, which creative to retire or promote; the rotation rules live in the plan, do not restate them), copy (which pain line or closing line is pulling; wording decisions pending), optimization (pacing, audience panel, placements, budget steps per the `scaling-playbook`; Meta's anomaly flags, peer percentiles, benchmark standing, auction overlap and the top opportunity-score recommendation from step 4b as evidence, cited by tool and window). Compare the chosen path with the alternative and name the weak link; raise a gap unprompted with a costed fix.
7. **Write and say.** The full report goes to `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-morning.md` with the report frontmatter below (never overwrite: a second run the same day is `-2`). The terminal gets at most 25 lines: health in one line, review and errors in one, spend and delivery in one, money vs caps in one (plus a flagged or red line per cap at 80 % or above), Meta's anomaly flags in one (count, and the entity, metric and direction of the first; or "none" / "no data"), the standing against peers and the industry benchmark in one (each ACTIVE ad set's cost-per-result percentile and its benchmark standing; or "no data"), the top three recommendations, and the two lists "Needs the Owner's go" and "Needs product team". Append one line to today's session note (`memory/sessions/YYYY-MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, from `memory/templates/session.md` if it does not exist): `- HH:MM — /morning: <phase>, <health colour>, Inbox <n> answered, report reports/YYYY-MM-DD-morning.md`.
8. **The HOT and the control-room board.** Run `timeout 120 node memory/tools/build-hot.mjs --by morning`. It regenerates `memory/HOT.md`, `memory/INDEX.md`, HOME's generated block and `dashboard/state.json` from the vault notes, which is why step 4 writes the read into `memory/state/current.md` and the campaign notes first. The board keeps the keys fixed by `docs/TEMPLATE-SPEC.md` §9 (`company`, `generatedAt`, `launch`, `routines` with the times from `config/operator.md`, `working`, `tasks` by owner as in `checklist.md`, `money`, `campaigns`, `team`, `skillsUsed`; the full shape with comments is in `control-room/README.md`): `money.dailySpend` and every other figure only from a live read (null when there is none); `money.caps` copied from the caps table of `config/goals.md` (the Owner's limits; `memory/business/goals.md` is the fallback while `config/goals.md` holds placeholders), as `[{"label": "daily" | "weekly" | "monthly" | "<campaign name>", "amount": <number>, "period": "day" | "week" | "month" | "campaign"}]` (`[]` while the caps are not set). When `memory/business/goals.md` disagrees with `config/goals.md`, `config/goals.md` wins: correct the note in the same run. Never hand-edit HOT, INDEX or `state.json`; if the script fails, say so in the terminal summary and leave the old files in place. Then run `node dashboard/setup-page.mjs` so the setup progress page (`dashboard/setup.html`) matches the board.

## Report template (fixed headings)
Frontmatter (vault contract `memory/README.md`, frontmatter):
```
---
type: report
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
status: done
maintained_by: harness
categories: [reports]
summary: Morning report YYYY-MM-DD, <phase>: <the one-line answer>.
campaign_ids: ["<every campaign id read in step 4>"]
as_of: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM<offset of the timezone in config/operator.md>
source: ads_get_ad_entities, ads_account_get_activity_logs, Meta diagnostics (step 4b)
---
```
Body:
```
# Morning report, YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM <timezone from config/operator.md>
Phase: pre-launch | live (flight N, day N of N) | switch day

## 1. Health            green / yellow / red lines from /health
## 2. Changed since the last session   activity log + differences from the vault notes + Inbox lines filed
## 3. Review and errors  pending / disapproved / with issues; ads_get_errors
## 4. Spend and delivery pre-launch: "0 <currency>, everything PAUSED"; live: the analyst's table, leads per ad set and where the Operator reads them
## 4a. Meta diagnostics  step 4b, the fixed table below (window last_7d; a yesterday column in the live phase); "no data" where a tool gave none
| Ad set | Main KPI | Percentile vs peers | Industry benchmark standing | Anomaly flags | Auction note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <name> (<id>) | cost per <result>: <value> | CPR pN · CTR pN · CPM pN | above / below benchmark on CPR, CTR, CPM | <metric> <spike/drop>, or none | stronger / weaker rank, the factor (bid, ad quality), overlap with <ad set>; or "one ACTIVE ad set" |
Account-level anomalies: <list, or none / no data>
Opportunity score: N/100; top recommendation: <name>, +N points, <url>   (or "Opportunity score: no data")
## 5. Money vs caps      each cap in config/goals.md: spend, % of the cap, green / flagged at 80 % / red above
## 6. Open gates by owner   Owner / Operator / Product team / Harness, from checklist.md
## 7. Recommendations    ranked; evidence → action → label
## 8. Needs the Owner's go (Inbox items included) · Needs the Owner's attention · Needs product team · Harness does now
```

## Breakage definitions and the click list the Owner gets
| Finding | How it shows | What the report gives the Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Disapproved ad or a policy notice (social issues, a special ad category) | `effective_status` DISAPPROVED / WITH_ISSUES, the activity log | The ad's name and id, the Ads Manager path (Ads → the ad → Review feedback), the harness's reading of the notice; no appeal, no declaration by an agent |
| Broken destination | `health` finds a non-200 or the wrong language on a destination page from `config/product.md` | The URL, the status, the request text for the product team |
| Pixel dark while ads run | no `PageView` in 24 h on the pixel (`pixel_id` in `config/accounts.md`) with an ACTIVE set; never raised when `pixel_id` is `none (no website)` | A `signals-audit` run offered, the consent-gating caveat stated when the pixel fires only after consent |
| Spend anomaly | spend over 120 % of a daily budget, or 0 with an ACTIVE set after 6 h | The set's name, budget, spend; the pause is the Owner's click (Ads Manager toggle) |
| Spend above a cap | money vs caps (step 5): spend above a cap in `config/goals.md`, or the live daily budgets summing above the daily cap | The cap, the spend and its period, the campaigns and ad sets that carry it, and the pause click list (Ads Manager → Campaigns → the toggle of each live campaign or ad set); the Owner pauses, or asks the harness to in chat |
| An object changed by another hand | the activity log (`event_category` `budget`, `status`, `targeting` first) | What changed, from and to, when, by which account label |
| Anomaly flagged by Meta | `ads_insights_anomaly_signal` (step 4b) | The entity (name and id), the metric, the direction (spike or drop) and the window; an observation to look into, not a cause. A read, never a pause: a pause, if the Owner wants one, is the Owner's click or an ask in chat |

## What this command is not
- Not the weekly creative round (`next-round`), not the session closer (`sync`), not a build. If the morning read calls for a build or a change, it ends as a labelled recommendation.

## Google Ads (when Google is a platform in `config/operator.md`)
After the Meta read, the same for Google: `timeout 120 node creatives/tools/google-ads.mjs report --level campaign --range YESTERDAY --json` and `--level ad --range TODAY` (statuses and approvals against the campaign notes with `platform: google`; a change by another hand is a finding), `--level search_term --range LAST_7_DAYS` once a week for new negatives. Spend from both platforms is added against the caps of `config/goals.md`, each platform against its share; 80 % of either is flagged. A breakage (a disapproval, an ENABLED campaign memory says is PAUSED) is reported with the click list or the `google-ads.mjs pause` line, and the Owner decides. Google has no anomaly, peer or benchmark tools here: those lines read "no data" for Google.
