## Forbidden words and claims

- "guaranteed" (in any form)
- "best price" or any price promise
- any investment, yield or return framing ("a great investment", "rental yield")
- "agency", "broker" or "brokerage" describing the company (it does not broker)
- any price trend or forecast ("prices will rise")

## Lines that need clearance before use in ads

- "Free for buyers, always" (French: "Gratuit pour les acheteurs, toujours"): the Owner confirms before use in ads.
